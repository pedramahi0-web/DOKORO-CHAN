Bokusatsu Tenshi Dokuro-chan

Bokusatsu Tenshi Dokuro-chan (撲殺天使ドクロちゃん), also known as Bludgeoning Angel Dokuro-chan, is a Japanese light novel series written by Masaki Okayama and illustrated by Torishimo. The series combines comedy, fantasy, and parody, and later received manga and anime adaptations.

Plot

The story follows Sakura Kusakabe, an ordinary middle school student whose life changes after meeting Dokuro Mitsukai, an angel from the future. Dokuro has come to prevent Sakura from creating a technology that would make girls stop aging at the age of twelve in the future. However, her unpredictable personality and unusual methods frequently lead to chaotic and comedic situations.

Characters

Dokuro Mitsukai: An energetic and eccentric angel from the future.

Sakura Kusakabe: A middle school student whose everyday life becomes increasingly chaotic after Dokuro arrives.

Sabato: Dokuro's magical spiked club, which is central to many of the series' comedic scenes.

Genres

Comedy, fantasy, parody, and supernatural fiction.

Adaptations

The franchise includes a light novel series, a manga adaptation, and an anime adaptation. The anime is particularly known for its exaggerated comedy, absurd situations, and over-the-top humor.
